**All content of Phases 1 to 4 is code-generated** (`game/Assets/Echoes/Scripts/Editor`): primitive-built characters (the caretaker, the hen, Mira, Wren and Alder), scenery, landmarks, gathering nodes, the garden, the windmill, lanterns, materials, small procedural textures (`TextureKit.cs`: ground tiles, water streaks, a soft particle dot, made from seeded noise), the gradient-sky shader (`EchoesGradientSky.shader`, own code), particle effects and a procedural terrain. No external art, audio, font or text file has been added in any phase; all dialogue, quest, ending and credits text is original and AI-written. It also uses resources that ship inside Unity itself and are not committed: the built-in primitive meshes, the built-in UI sprites `UI/Skin/UISprite.psd` and `UI/Skin/Knob.psd`, the built-in font `LegacyRuntime.ttf` and Unity's URP shaders. Inspecting a built player's data shows that the `LegacyRuntime` font object holds **no font file** (it names the operating system's Arial), so no font file ships in the release package; that is the assistant's reading of the binary, not documentation. Unity's own splash screen is left on. Confirm the Unity Personal terms and the font behaviour before submission (open item).

**Audio (Phase 4) is original and synthesised by code; nothing is sampled, recorded, downloaded or taken from a library.** The 36 WAV files in `game/Assets/Echoes/Audio/{Music,Ambience,Sfx}` (16-bit PCM mono, 22.05 or 32 kHz, about 20 MB) are written by `Scripts/Editor/AudioSynth.cs` (additive and modal synthesis of bells, harp plucks, marimba, pads, a flute-like lead, filtered noise for wind, water, birds and footsteps, and a reverb made by convolving with a synthetic decaying-noise impulse response) and `Scripts/Editor/AudioLibrary.cs` (the composition and sound design). The generation is deterministic (seeded), so the same code writes the same files. The music is one short piece in D major at 80 beats per minute: a four-chord loop (D, A over C#, B minor 7, G major 7) split into layers that the game fades in per restoration stage, two eight-bar melody phrases, and a 43-second ending piece. The melody and arrangement were written note by note by the assistant; to the assistant's knowledge they copy no existing piece (the chord loop itself is a common progression), but nobody has *listened* to them for accidental resemblance and nobody has judged their quality: both are **pending human review**. **The official theme recording is not used in the game, the repository or the build, and nothing in the music samples, remixes, transcribes or resynthesises it.**

Hold packs with unclear license (including conflicting Quaternius/QAL claims) until verified. Prefer owned/CC0 art; avoid noncommercial and ripped content. Do not mistake a license allowing game use for permission to publish raw source assets.
